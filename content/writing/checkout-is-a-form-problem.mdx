Teams spend weeks debating trust badges and express-pay button order while
the actual leak is a shipping-address field that fights browser autofill on
mobile Safari.

Before touching the visual design of a checkout flow, watch five real
sessions on a real phone. The friction is almost always mechanical: a field
that resets on autocomplete, a required field with no visible reason, a
keyboard type mismatch.
