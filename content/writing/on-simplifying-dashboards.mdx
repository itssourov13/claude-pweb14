Most dashboards don't fail on visual design. They fail because they try to
answer every question a stakeholder ever asked in one meeting, at once, on
one screen.

The fix isn't a better chart library. It's asking the three questions a user
actually returns for, building the home view around only those, and moving
everything else one click away — which feels like a loss until the usage
numbers come back.
