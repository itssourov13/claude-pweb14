## Problem

Atlas's dashboard had grown to 30+ configurable widgets and users were
overwhelmed; usage was concentrated on three metrics buried in the noise.

## Approach

I interviewed a dozen customers, found the three questions everyone actually
asked, and rebuilt the home dashboard around those, with everything else
moved one click deeper.

## Outcome

Weekly active usage rose 31% and dashboard-related support tickets fell by
half within the following quarter.

## Learnings

Simplification is a design decision, not a settings toggle — offering both
the simple and dense views as defaults would have just recreated the
problem.
