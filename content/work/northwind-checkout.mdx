## Problem

Northwind's multi-step checkout was losing a quarter of buyers between cart
and confirmation, most of them on the shipping-details step on mobile.

## Approach

I collapsed the three-step flow into a single scrolling page with inline
validation, added express payment buttons above the fold, and rebuilt the
address form around autofill instead of fighting it.

## Outcome

Abandonment dropped 23% within the first month post-launch, with the biggest
gain on mobile Safari, where autofill friction had been worst.

## Learnings

Most of the win came from removing steps, not adding polish — the fancier
animation work mattered far less than the form actually being fillable.
